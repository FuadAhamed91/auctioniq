# How each franchise buys: documented habits before the 2026 auction

Every source below was published **on or before 15 December 2025**, the day before the IPL 2026
mini-auction (16 December 2025, Abu Dhabi), and each date was checked on the page itself. Nothing here
uses or refers to anything from that auction or later. Where a page also carried later material
(sidebars, related links), only its own dated article text was used.

- **Fact**: a documented event, price or number.
- **Stated**: the on-the-record words of the franchise's owner, executive, coach or captain. That it was
  said is documented; what it says is the club's own view.
- **Opinion**: a journalist's or pundit's assessment.

ESPNcricinfo stories are linked through ESPN's mirror pages (africa.espn.com, global.espn.com) because
espncricinfo.com refused automated access; text and bylines are ESPNcricinfo's. The "2025 mega auction"
took place in November 2024 and the "2024 auction" in December 2023. The 2026 purses, slots and squad
counts are from the official retention announcement of 15 November 2025. These notes are background for
the team panels; they do not change how the agents bid.

## CSK (Chennai Super Kings)

- **Fact.** The 2018 squad averaged 29 years, with 11 players aged 30 or over, and averaged 33 after the
  first day of that auction [IBTimes India, 7 Apr 2018](https://www.ibtimes.co.in/ipl-2018-stephen-fleming-reveals-why-chennai-super-kings-prefer-old-team-766048);
  the XI in the 2018 final "averaged almost 31 years" [The Quint, 28 May 2018](https://www.thequint.com/amp/story/sports/cricket/ipl-2018-final-csk-dhoni-dads-army).
- **Stated.** Coach Stephen Fleming: "We have really valued experience", with "a multi-skilled team in our ranks"
  [DT Next, 29 Jan 2018](https://www.dtnext.in/sports/2018/01/29/chennai-super-kings-is-a-multiskilled-team-stephen-fleming);
  "I'm not here to develop young players, I'm here to try and win the trophy for the franchise"
  [Wisden, 25 Apr 2018](https://www.wisden.com/cricket-news/win-trophy-not-develop-players-stephen-fleming).
- **Stated.** On method, Fleming: "We don't get too deep into analytics and numbers; we go with gut feel and
  developing relationships with players" [DT Next, 16 Oct 2021](https://www.dtnext.in/sports/2021/10/16/we-go-with-gut-feel-fleming-spells-csks-winning-mantra).
- **Fact.** Former players bought back: Deepak Chahar (₹14 Cr), Ambati Rayudu (₹6.75 Cr) and Dwayne Bravo at the
  2022 mega auction [The Quint, 14 Feb 2022](https://www.thequint.com/indian-premier-league-ipl/ipl-auction-2022-chennai-super-kings-rebuild-in-their-own-special-way-again);
  Shardul Thakur (₹4 Cr) for a second stint [ESPNcricinfo, 19 Dec 2023](https://africa.espn.com/cricket/story/_/id/39144832/ipl-2024-auction-mitchell-starc-travis-head-rachin-ravindra-shardul-thakur-go-hammer);
  R Ashwin (₹9.75 Cr) and Rachin Ravindra via RTM [ESPNcricinfo, 24 Nov 2024](https://global.espn.com/cricket/story/_/id/42578844/ipl-auction-2025-shreyas-iyer-first-player-breach-inr-25-crore-mark-ipl-auction),
  with Sam Curran and Vijay Shankar also brought back [ESPNcricinfo, 17 Mar 2025](https://africa.espn.com/cricket/story/_/id/44284185/csk-look-ready-spin-win-again-chennai).
  **Opinion:** CSK "have perennially stuck to the same core, auction after auction" (The Quint, above).
- **Fact / Opinion.** Spin for Chepauk: CSK bought Harbhajan Singh, Karn Sharma, Imran Tahir and Mitchell Santner in 2018
  (DT Next, 29 Jan 2018, above) and paid ₹10 Cr for Noor Ahmad in 2024, which ESPNcricinfo read as a return to
  "spin-to-win" at Chepauk, although pacers took 74 wickets to the spinners' 25 there in IPL 2024
  [ESPNcricinfo, 17 Mar 2025](https://africa.espn.com/cricket/story/_/id/44284185/csk-look-ready-spin-win-again-chennai).
  **Stated:** Fleming later said "It's not the Chepauk [of old] where you can just go in and play four spinners"
  [IANS, 29 Mar 2025](https://ianslive.in/ipl-2025-there-was-no-home-advantage-at-chepauk-for-csk-says-fleming--20250329093444).
- **Fact / Stated.** Not only veterans: CSK paid ₹8.40 Cr for uncapped Sameer Rizvi and ₹14 Cr for Daryl Mitchell at the
  December 2023 auction (ESPNcricinfo, 19 Dec 2023, above). In 2025 Fleming said poor results had pushed CSK, who
  had traditionally relied on experienced players, to use the whole squad; 17-year-old Ayush Mhatre and 21-year-old
  Dewald Brevis were signed as replacements [ESPNcricinfo, 19 Apr 2025](https://africa.espn.com/cricket/story/_/id/44748905/ipl-2025-stephen-fleming-admits-poor-results-forced-csk-bring-all-squad-members-play).
  MS Dhoni: "there were certain holes that we needed to plug in", with "a small auction ... coming in December"
  [Free Press Journal, 3 Aug 2025](https://www.freepressjournal.in/sports/need-to-plug-certain-holes-ms-dhoni-reveals-chennai-super-kings-future-plans).
- **Opinion.** CSK's "focus on continuity and experienced cricketers has helped them win five titles"
  [CricTracker, 7 Dec 2025](https://www.crictracker.com/cricket-appeal/auction-patterns-of-every-ipl-franchise-what-they-never-change-3804/10).
- **2026 needs as reported before the auction.** Sanju Samson arrived by trade (₹18 Cr) for Ravindra Jadeja (fee revised to
  ₹14 Cr) and Sam Curran [ESPNcricinfo, 15 Nov 2025](https://africa.espn.com/cricket/story/_/id/46971443/ipl-2026-rr-trade-samson-csk-jadeja-curran);
  purse ₹43.40 Cr, 9 slots (4 overseas) [IPLT20, 15 Nov 2025](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2026-player-retentions-announced).
  Top of the list: an elite overseas all-rounder or finisher, with overseas fast bowlers as the fallback, and a
  replacement for Jadeja's all-round spin with no like-for-like domestic option [ESPNcricinfo, 12 Dec 2025](https://africa.espn.com/cricket/story/_/id/47282197/ipl-2026-auction-csk-dc-gt-kkr-lsg-mi-pbks-rr-rcb-srh-want-their-squads);
  also a seam back-up for Nathan Ellis [ESPNcricinfo, 15 Nov 2025](https://africa.espn.com/cricket/story/_/id/46973801/ipl-2026-how-squads-stack-up).

## DC (Delhi Capitals)

- **Fact.** With Ricky Ponting as coach in 2018 the franchise "has taken a much more formal approach to team building",
  retaining Rishabh Pant and Shreyas Iyer and making big batting buys (Gautam Gambhir, Glenn Maxwell, Colin Munro, Jason Roy)
  [DNA, 28 Jan 2018](https://www.dnaindia.com/cricket/report-ipl-auctions-2018-coach-ponting-does-the-trick-dd-get-auction-strategy-right-on-in-day-1-2579047).
- **Opinion.** Delhi's strength was "the young Indian core, undoubtedly": Prithvi Shaw, Shreyas Iyer and Rishabh Pant; the
  same piece flagged a lack of Indian pacers [Scroll, 19 Nov 2018](https://scroll.in/field/902701/ipl-2019-retentions-delhi-daredevils-in-transition-once-again-but-their-indian-core-is-exciting).
- **Stated.** Co-owner Parth Jindal on not matching ₹27 Cr for Pant: DC were prepared to go to about ₹22-23 Cr, the bidding
  for Shreyas Iyer "had crossed all budgets", and paying more would have left too little "for building the rest of the
  squad"; DC needed one marquee Indian batter [ESPNcricinfo, 27 Nov 2024](https://africa.espn.com/cricket/story/_/id/42638621/ipl-auction-2025-parth-jindal-why-delhi-capitals-did-not-retain-pant-kl-rahul-captaincy).
  **Fact:** they bought KL Rahul (₹14 Cr) and Mitchell Starc (₹11.75 Cr) and used RTM to keep Jake Fraser-McGurk
  [ESPNcricinfo, 24 Nov 2024](https://global.espn.com/cricket/story/_/id/42578844/ipl-auction-2025-shreyas-iyer-first-player-breach-inr-25-crore-mark-ipl-auction).
- **Stated.** Chairman Kiran Kumar Grandhi: "we did a lot of scouting, mock auctions" and "We went as per our plans, and
  budgets"; head coach Hemang Badani: "a good Indian core" [Delhi Capitals, 28 Nov 2024](https://www.delhicapitals.in/news/happy-with-the-squad-delhi-capitals-management-credits-strategic-preparation-for-ipl-2025-mega-auction-success).
- **Fact.** Retained before the 2025 mega auction: Axar Patel, Kuldeep Yadav, Tristan Stubbs, Abishek Porel (₹73 Cr left)
  [IPLT20, 1 Nov 2024](https://www.iplt20.com/news/4110/tata-ipl-2025-player-retentions-list-announced).
- **Opinion.** DC favour "identifying high-potential domestic players and backing them early", and their "long-term
  investment philosophy remains unchanged" [CricTracker, 7 Dec 2025](https://www.crictracker.com/cricket-appeal/auction-patterns-of-every-ipl-franchise-what-they-never-change-3804/5).
- **2026 needs as reported before the auction.** Purse ₹21.80 Cr, 8 slots (5 overseas) [IPLT20, 15 Nov 2025](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2026-player-retentions-announced);
  Nitish Rana arrived from RR in a trade for Donovan Ferreira [IANS, 15 Dec 2025](https://ianslive.in/ipl-2026-auction-clear-about-our-targets-to-complete-our-squad-says-dc-ceo--20251215172542).
  Needs: a reliable opener to partner KL Rahul after
  Jake Fraser-McGurk and Faf du Plessis left, back-up seamers, and a middle-order overseas batter or all-rounder
  [ESPNcricinfo, 12 Dec 2025](https://africa.espn.com/cricket/story/_/id/47282197/ipl-2026-auction-csk-dc-gt-kkr-lsg-mi-pbks-rr-rcb-srh-want-their-squads).
  **Stated:** CEO Sunil Gupta: "we are clear about our targets", DC will be "aggressive where needed" and
  "flexibility is key" [IANS, 15 Dec 2025](https://ianslive.in/ipl-2026-auction-clear-about-our-targets-to-complete-our-squad-says-dc-ceo--20251215172542).

## GT (Gujarat Titans)

- **Stated.** Head coach Ashish Nehra before the debut season: "When you talk about T20 format you need all-rounders and
  we have done that"; the squad is "a combination of youngsters along with experience"
  [Outlook India, 15 Feb 2022](https://www.outlookindia.com/sports/ipl-auction-2022-success-depends-on-how-we-gel-as-a-unit-says-gujarat-titans-coach-ashish-nehra-news-182440).
- **Fact.** First auction (2022): Rahul Tewatia ₹9 Cr, David Miller ₹3 Cr, Matthew Wade ₹2.4 Cr, Wriddhiman Saha ₹1.9 Cr,
  plus Lockie Ferguson [ESPNcricinfo, 13 Feb 2022](https://africa.espn.com/cricket/story/_/id/33279411/ipl-2022-auction-liam-livingstone-aiden-markram-ajinkya-rahane-sold-csk-mi-kkr-rcb-dc-pbks-srh-lsg-gt-rr).
- **Fact.** Retained before the 2025 mega auction: Rashid Khan, Shubman Gill, Sai Sudharsan, Rahul Tewatia, Shahrukh Khan
  [IPLT20, 1 Nov 2024](https://www.iplt20.com/news/4110/tata-ipl-2025-player-retentions-list-announced);
  then Jos Buttler ₹15.75 Cr, Mohammed Siraj ₹12.25 Cr and Kagiso Rabada ₹10.75 Cr
  [ESPNcricinfo, 24 Nov 2024](https://global.espn.com/cricket/story/_/id/42578844/ipl-auction-2025-shreyas-iyer-first-player-breach-inr-25-crore-mark-ipl-auction).
- **Opinion.** GT's top three "like to take the innings deep", the middle order lost Miller, and Siraj, Rabada and Prasidh
  Krishna "have a tendency to go for runs" [Wisden, 26 Nov 2024](https://www.wisden.com/series/ipl-2025/cricket-news/rcb-at-the-top-ranking-each-squad-after-ipl-2025-mega-auction).
- **Opinion.** "GT have consistently invested in all-rounders such as Hardik Pandya, Rashid Khan, Washington Sundar, and
  Rahul Tewatia" [CricTracker, 7 Dec 2025](https://www.crictracker.com/cricket-appeal/auction-patterns-of-every-ipl-franchise-what-they-never-change-3804/2).
- **Fact.** Ownership: Torrent Group agreed to buy 67% from CVC, which keeps 33%
  [Gujarat Titans, 12 Feb 2025](https://gujarattitansipl.com/news/torrent-group-acquires-gujarat-titans).
- **Stated.** On the 2026 retentions, director of cricket Vikram Solanki: the aim was to "secure a core that aligns with
  the vision of Gujarat Titans", with continuity and balance; Nehra: the group "gives us balance and clarity"
  [The Tribune (ANI), 15 Nov 2025](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/sports/gujarat-titans-announce-retained-and-released-players-ahead-of-ipl-2026-auction).
- **2026 needs as reported before the auction.** Released Dasun Shanaka, Mahipal Lomror, Karim Janat, Gerald Coetzee and
  Kulwant Khejroliya; Sherfane Rutherford traded to MI (The Tribune, above); purse ₹12.90 Cr, 5 slots (4 overseas)
  [IPLT20, 15 Nov 2025](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2026-player-retentions-announced).
  Needs: middle-order batters or finishers, an overseas bowling partner or back-up for Rabada, and an overseas spin-bowling
  all-rounder [ESPNcricinfo, 12 Dec 2025](https://africa.espn.com/cricket/story/_/id/47282197/ipl-2026-auction-csk-dc-gt-kkr-lsg-mi-pbks-rr-rcb-srh-want-their-squads).

## KKR (Kolkata Knight Riders)

- **Fact.** Top prices for overseas fast bowlers: Pat Cummins ₹15.50 Cr in 2020, and Mitchell Starc ₹24.75 Cr at the
  December 2023 auction, then the record [ESPNcricinfo, 19 Dec 2023](https://africa.espn.com/cricket/story/_/id/39144832/ipl-2024-auction-mitchell-starc-travis-head-rachin-ravindra-shardul-thakur-go-hammer).
- **Stated.** CEO Venky Mysore: Starc "was a preferred player from skill set standpoint"; every team decides how to slice
  its purse, so a price is "a matter of perspective" [Outlook India, 19 Dec 2023](https://www.outlookindia.com/sports/ipl-2024-player-auction-why-mitchell-starc-bagged-record-breaking-inr-24-75-crore-deal-kolkata-knight-riders-ceo-venky-mysore-explains-rationale-news-337513).
- **Fact / Stated.** Keeping the 2024 title core: six retained (Rinku Singh, Varun Chakaravarthy, Sunil Narine, Andre
  Russell, Harshit Rana, Ramandeep Singh) [IPLT20, 1 Nov 2024](https://www.iplt20.com/news/4110/tata-ipl-2025-player-retentions-list-announced),
  then Venkatesh Iyer bought back for ₹23.75 Cr; Mysore: "For us it was about trying to keep our core"
  [ESPNcricinfo, 24 Nov 2024](https://africa.espn.com/cricket/story/_/id/42583070/ipl-2025-auction-kkr-splurging-venkatesh-iyer-auctions-way-only).
- **Fact.** Mystery spin: KKR paid ₹4 Cr for uncapped Varun Chakaravarthy in December 2019 as "mystery backup" for Narine
  [Scroll, 20 Dec 2019](https://scroll.in/field/947382/ipl-2020-auction-varun-chakaravarthy-yashasvi-jaiswal-lead-the-charge-of-indias-uncapped-players).
- **Stated.** Captain Ajinkya Rahane after a home defeat: "We would love to see a pitch that helps spin bowlers"
  [WION, 23 Mar 2025](https://www.wionews.com/cricket/ipl-2025-rahane-calls-for-spin-friendly-pitch-at-eden-gardens-after-kkrs-loss-to-rcb-8881652).
- **Opinion.** "KKR prefer players who can single-handedly win games", even when the risk is high
  [CricTracker, 7 Dec 2025](https://www.crictracker.com/cricket-appeal/auction-patterns-of-every-ipl-franchise-what-they-never-change-3804/7).
- **Fact.** For 2026, Abhishek Nayar, with KKR's coaching set-up since 2018, replaced Chandrakant Pandit as head coach
  [The Week, 30 Oct 2025](https://www.theweek.in/news/sports/2025/10/30/kolkata-knight-riders-appoint-abhishek-nayar-as-head-coach.html);
  Russell, released, retired from the IPL and joined KKR as "Power Coach" [Reuters via The Star, 30 Nov 2025](https://www.thestar.com.my/sport/cricket/2025/11/30/cricket-russell-retires-from-ipl-joins-kkr039s-coaching-department).
- **2026 needs as reported before the auction.** Biggest purse, ₹64.30 Cr, and most slots, 13 (6 overseas)
  [IPLT20, 15 Nov 2025](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2026-player-retentions-announced),
  after releasing Russell, Quinton de Kock and others [ESPNcricinfo, 15 Nov 2025](https://africa.espn.com/cricket/story/_/id/46973801/ipl-2026-how-squads-stack-up).
  Needs: an explosive overseas opener who can keep wicket, one or two overseas fast bowlers, an Indian keeper, and a big
  overseas all-rounder to replace Russell [ESPNcricinfo, 12 Dec 2025](https://africa.espn.com/cricket/story/_/id/47282197/ipl-2026-auction-csk-dc-gt-kkr-lsg-mi-pbks-rr-rcb-srh-want-their-squads).

## LSG (Lucknow Super Giants)

- **Fact.** In their first auction LSG paid ₹10 Cr for Avesh Khan, then the most expensive uncapped player in IPL history
  [The Quint, 12 Feb 2022](https://thequint.com/indian-premier-league-ipl/ipl-auction-2022-bidding-war-ends-with-avesh-khan-going-to-lucknow-for-rs-10-crore).
- **Fact / Stated.** Rishabh Pant for a record ₹27 Cr after DC's RTM [ESPNcricinfo, 24 Nov 2024](https://global.espn.com/cricket/story/_/id/42578844/ipl-auction-2025-shreyas-iyer-first-player-breach-inr-25-crore-mark-ipl-auction).
  Owner Sanjiv Goenka: "For Rishabh, we had calculated three scenarios, and in all of them, he was very important to us";
  the report says LSG had earmarked ₹25-27 Cr [Business Today, 13 Dec 2024](https://businesstoday.in/sports/ipl/story/he-was-very-important-to-us-sanjeev-goenka-on-lsgs-record-rs27-crore-bid-for-rishabh-pant-in-ipl-auction-457173-2024-12-13).
- **Fact.** Retained before the 2025 mega auction: Nicholas Pooran, Ravi Bishnoi, Mayank Yadav, Mohsin Khan, Ayush Badoni
  [IPLT20, 1 Nov 2024](https://www.iplt20.com/news/4110/tata-ipl-2025-player-retentions-list-announced).
  **Opinion:** "one of the strongest attacks" and an all-Indian core [Wisden, 26 Nov 2024](https://www.wisden.com/series/ipl-2025/cricket-news/rcb-at-the-top-ranking-each-squad-after-ipl-2025-mega-auction).
- **Stated.** Mentor Zaheer Khan on the Ekana pitch: the curator "is not really thinking that it's a home game"
  [Outlook India (PTI), 2 Apr 2025](https://www.outlookindia.com/sports/cricket/lsg-vs-pbks-ipl-2025-zaheer-khan-slams-lucknows-pitch-looked-like-punjabs-curator-was-out-here).
- **Fact.** Tom Moody became Global Director of Cricket for the RPSG teams, overseeing cricket strategy, the player auction,
  retentions and global talent scouting, alongside head coach Justin Langer and strategic advisor Kane Williamson
  [Lucknow Super Giants, 4 Nov 2025](https://www.lucknowsupergiants.in/news/lucknow-super-giants-lsg-appoint-tom-moody-global-director-of-cricket).
- **Fact.** For 2026 LSG kept "most of their core", including Mayank Yadav despite back surgery, and released Bishnoi,
  David Miller and Akash Deep [ESPNcricinfo, 14 Nov 2025](https://africa.espn.com/cricket/story/_/id/46965820/ipl-2026-lsg-set-retain-mayank-yadav-release-ravi-bishnoi-david-miller-akash-deep);
  Mohammed Shami arrived by trade from SRH [IANS, 15 Nov 2025](https://ianslive.in/ipl-2026-sunrisers-hyderabad-retain-core-release-zampa-mulder-and-five-others--20251115200459)
  and Shardul Thakur left for MI [Onmanorama, 13 Nov 2025](https://www.onmanorama.com/sports/cricket/2025/11/13/ipl-mumbai-indians-secure-shardul-thakur-sherfane-rutherford-before-auction.amp.html).
- **Opinion.** LSG buy by role, prioritising experienced all-rounders, power-hitters and reliable bowlers
  [CricTracker, 7 Dec 2025](https://www.crictracker.com/cricket-appeal/auction-patterns-of-every-ipl-franchise-what-they-never-change-3804/).
- **2026 needs as reported before the auction.** Purse ₹22.95 Cr, 6 slots (4 overseas)
  [IPLT20, 15 Nov 2025](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2026-player-retentions-announced).
  Needs: an overseas finisher for the middle order, Indian back-up seamers while the main quicks recover from injury, and a
  spinner to replace Bishnoi [ESPNcricinfo, 12 Dec 2025](https://africa.espn.com/cricket/story/_/id/47282197/ipl-2026-auction-csk-dc-gt-kkr-lsg-mi-pbks-rr-rcb-srh-want-their-squads).

## MI (Mumbai Indians)

- **Stated.** Owner Nita Ambani: "To back youngsters has been MI's philosophy since 2010", citing Hardik Pandya, Jasprit
  Bumrah and Krunal Pandya as uncapped players who grew with MI; most of MI's other buys at that auction were uncapped
  Indians [Gulf News (IANS), 20 Dec 2019](https://gulfnews.com/amp/sport/cricket/nita-ambani-says-to-back-youngsters-is-mumbai-indians-philosophy-1.1576833759548).
- **Stated.** Captain Hardik Pandya on seamer Ashwani Kumar's debut: "It's all the scouts, they picked him"
  [Reuters via The Star, 1 Apr 2025](https://www.thestar.com.my/sport/cricket/2025/04/01/cricket-mumbai-scouts-find-another-ipl-gem-in-paceman-ashwani).
- **Fact / Stated.** At the 2022 mega auction MI bought back Ishan Kishan for ₹15.25 Cr and paid ₹8 Cr for Jofra Archer
  [ESPNcricinfo, 13 Feb 2022](https://africa.espn.com/cricket/story/_/id/33279411/ipl-2022-auction-liam-livingstone-aiden-markram-ajinkya-rahane-sold-csk-mi-kkr-rcb-dc-pbks-srh-lsg-gt-rr),
  who was not available that year; Akash Ambani said MI "bid for all the fast bowlers before him", were "priced out of all
  of them", and Archer was the "only marquee fast bowler left" [The Quint (IANS), 13 Feb 2022](https://www.thequint.com/indian-premier-league-ipl/ipl-2022-auction-was-clear-jofra-was-only-marquee-pacer-left-says-akash-ambani).
- **Fact.** Retained before the 2025 mega auction: Jasprit Bumrah, Suryakumar Yadav, Hardik Pandya, Rohit Sharma, Tilak
  Varma [IPLT20, 1 Nov 2024](https://www.iplt20.com/news/4110/tata-ipl-2025-player-retentions-list-announced);
  Naman Dhir kept via RTM [ESPNcricinfo, 24 Nov 2024](https://global.espn.com/cricket/story/_/id/42578844/ipl-auction-2025-shreyas-iyer-first-player-breach-inr-25-crore-mark-ipl-auction).
- **Stated.** Head coach Mahela Jayawardene: "Consistency is key and we built our team around a core group of players and
  they still remain"; the 2026 trades brought back Sherfane Rutherford, who had been with MI before, and Mayank Markande,
  part of an MI title-winning side, plus Mumbai local Shardul Thakur [CricTracker, 15 Nov 2025](https://www.crictracker.com/cricket-news/ipl-2026-mahela-jayawardene-explains-mumbai-indians-reasoning-behind-retentions-and-trades/?amp=1);
  "We've combined experienced international players like Mitchell Santner and Will Jacks with young talent"
  [The Tribune (ANI), 5 Dec 2025](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/sports/weve-built-our-team-around-a-core-group-of-hungry-players-mi-head-coach-mahela-jayawardene).
- **Opinion.** MI's pipeline of "unheralded talents" (Hardik Pandya, Bumrah, Tilak Varma, Suryakumar Yadav) is one of the
  most successful auction strategies in IPL history [CricTracker, 7 Dec 2025](https://www.crictracker.com/cricket-appeal/auction-patterns-of-every-ipl-franchise-what-they-never-change-3804/9).
- **2026 needs as reported before the auction.** Smallest purse, ₹2.75 Cr, 5 slots (1 overseas)
  [IPLT20, 15 Nov 2025](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2026-player-retentions-announced);
  "all bases covered", so no big buys expected [ESPNcricinfo, 15 Nov 2025](https://africa.espn.com/cricket/story/_/id/46973801/ipl-2026-how-squads-stack-up).
